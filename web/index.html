Operations-focused Android software for maintenance requests, interview scheduling, procurement requests, and patient record notes.

View the workspace Contact support

Four business lanes in one local app.

Teams can record the daily operational details that often move between paper, messages, and spreadsheets, while keeping the information on the Android device.

M Maintenance requests

Capture facility issues, priority, responsible contact, department, notes, and target date.

I Interview scheduling

Track candidate panels, room readiness, contacts, and upcoming interview tasks.

P Procurement requests

Record supply needs, purchasing follow-up, priority items, and status in one queue.

R Patient records

Keep local patient service notes for intake, updates, follow-up, and file readiness.

Built for daily operators.

The Android app opens directly to active work, then lets staff create, review, complete, and clear local records without registration or external services.

1
Create a record

Select the business lane, add the responsible contact, department, date, and notes.

2
Review the queue

See priority work first and open detail pages for each saved item.

3
Close the loop

Mark completed tasks, update notes, or clear local records from settings.

Private by default.

PETERSON CHRISTOPHER does not require an account, does not use analytics, and does not upload records. Saved information remains in local Android app storage until the user clears app data or uninstalls the app.
